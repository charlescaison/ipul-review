What to expect

Our services are straightforward. Jesus is at the center of all of it.

When you visit, you can expect:

  • songs of worship
  • prayer, together and for one another
  • a message from the Bible
  • someone to pray with you, if you would like

Whatever you are carrying, you are welcome here. We look forward to meeting you.
