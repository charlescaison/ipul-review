Baptism in the name of Jesus Christ

Baptism is one of the commandments the Holy Spirit gave in the book of Acts. We baptize the way the believers in the book of Acts were baptized: in water, in the name of Jesus Christ.

  • What the Bible says
  • What it means
  • If you want to be baptized

What the Bible says

What it means

Peter's words give three steps:

  1. Repent
  2. Be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of sins
  3. Receive the gift of the Holy Spirit

The promise is for you, for your children, and for everyone far away, as many as the Lord our God will call.

The promise is for you.
